Privacy Policy

Kevin Nguyen built the Japanese Dictionary app (formerly "JDict") as a free app. This service is provided by NDC at no cost and is intended for use as-is. By using this app, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

Last updated: October 12, 2026


1. Information Collection and Use

Japanese Dictionary does not request or collect your microphone, camera, location, contacts, SMS, or storage access. The app does not require an account or sign-in.

Notes, favorite vocabulary lists, and search history are stored locally on your device only - this data is never uploaded to us or to any server we control.

AI Tutor / AI Assistant (optional): if you use these features, your questions are sent to an AI provider (Google Gemini by default) to generate a response. A random, locally-generated device identifier - not tied to your name, email, or any other personal identity - is sent along to apply a fair-use rate limit for the free daily quota. If you add your own personal API key in Settings, requests are sent using that key instead, subject to that provider's own privacy policy.

Purchases: if you buy the ad-free upgrade, the purchase is processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment details.


2. Advertising & Analytics

We use third-party services that may collect information used to identify you:

Google AdMob - shows ads within the app. AdMob may collect and use an advertising identifier (Advertising ID) to show relevant ads and measure ad performance.

Google Analytics for Firebase - collects anonymous usage statistics (e.g. which screens are used) to help us understand app usage and improve it.

Firebase Crashlytics - collects crash reports and basic diagnostic information (such as device model, operating system version, and the state of the app when it crashed) to help us find and fix bugs. It does not identify you personally.

Install Referrer - used to attribute app installs to ad campaigns; does not identify you personally.

See Google's Advertising Policies (policies.google.com/technologies/ads) and the Google Play Services Privacy Policy (policies.google.com/privacy) for details.


3. Children's Privacy

This service does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so it can be removed.


4. Security

We value your trust in providing your information, and we strive to use commercially acceptable means of protecting it. Remember, however, that no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.
