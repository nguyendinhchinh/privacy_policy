# Privacy Policy for Japanese Dictionary

**Last updated: October 12, 2026**

Kevin Nguyen built the Japanese Dictionary app (formerly "JDict") as a free app. This SERVICE is
provided by NDC at no cost and is intended for use as is.

This page informs visitors of my policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of
Personal Information for anyone using my Service.

If you choose to use my Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in relation
to this policy. The Personal Information I collect is used to provide and improve the Service. I
will not use or share your information with anyone except as described in this Privacy Policy.

## Information Collection and Use

Japanese Dictionary does **not** request or collect your microphone, camera, location, contacts,
SMS, or storage access. The app does not require an account or sign-in.

- **Notes, favorite vocabulary lists, and search history** are stored locally on your device only.
  This data is never uploaded to me or to any server I control.
- **AI Assistant feature (optional):** if you choose to use the AI Assistant, your questions are
  sent directly to Google's Gemini API to generate a response. A random, locally-generated device
  identifier (not tied to your name, email, or any other personal identity) is sent along with the
  request, used only to apply a fair-use rate limit. If you provide your own personal Gemini API
  key in Settings, requests are sent using that key instead, subject to Google's own privacy
  policy for the Gemini API.
- **Purchases:** if you buy the ad-free upgrade, the purchase is processed entirely by Google Play
  Billing. I do not receive or store your payment details.

## Log Data and Advertising

I use third-party services that may collect information used to identify you:

- **Google AdMob** — shows ads within the app. AdMob may collect and use an advertising
  identifier (Advertising ID) to show ads relevant to you and to measure ad performance. See
  [Google's Advertising Policies](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads) for details, and
  [Google Play Services Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Google Analytics for Firebase** — collects anonymous usage statistics (e.g. which screens are
  used) to help me understand how the app is used and improve it.
- **Firebase Crashlytics** — collects crash reports and basic diagnostic information (such as device model,
  operating system version, and the state of the app when it crashed) to help me find and fix bugs. It does not
  identify you personally.
- **Install Referrer** — used to attribute app installs to ad campaigns; does not identify you
  personally.

## Children's Privacy

information, please contact me so it can be removed.
